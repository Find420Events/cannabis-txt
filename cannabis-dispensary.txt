======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

cannabis-dispensary.txt is a proposed, human-readable extension for
 describing cannabis retail dispensaries and the public-facing services,
 locations, products, and operations associated with them.

It establishes a shared vocabulary for describing dispensary businesses,
physical storefronts, retail access, product categories, menus, inventory
availability, customer services, and business relationships.

This extension complements cannabis.txt. It does not replace official
business websites, menus, point-of-sale systems, inventory feeds, APIs,
structured data, or regulatory records.

Publication does not guarantee search ranking, AI retrieval, business
verification, licensing, or inclusion in any directory or recommendation.

======================================================================
2. SCOPE
======================================================================

This extension covers public business information about cannabis retail
operations, including:

- Adult-use / recreational retail, where authorized
- Medical cannabis retail, where authorized
- Hybrid medical and adult-use retail, where authorized
- Physical dispensary storefronts
- Retail chains and multi-location operators
- Retail menus and product categories
- Publicly stated inventory and availability
- In-store shopping, pickup, and customer services
- Store hours, accessibility, and location information
- Retail promotions and loyalty programs, where lawful

A dispensary may operate one or more locations and may also offer delivery.
Delivery operations should additionally be described using
cannabis-delivery.txt when applicable.

This file describes business information; it does not establish that a
business is legally authorized to conduct any regulated activity.

======================================================================
3. CONFORMANCE
======================================================================

A compatible file SHOULD:

- Be published at /cannabis-dispensary.txt on the publisher's domain.
- Be served as UTF-8 plain text (text/plain; charset=utf-8).
- Identify version, status, publisher, canonical URL, and last-updated date.
- Declare the compatible cannabis.txt parent version.
- Identify whether information describes the organization, a location,
  a brand, or a third-party listing.
- Use the shared vocabulary in cannabis.txt where practical.
- Link to authoritative public pages for locations, menus, policies, and
  licensing information where appropriate.
- Mark information that is unknown, estimated, or subject to change.
- State the jurisdiction and effective date for legal or licensing claims.

A compatible file MUST NOT:

- Claim that publication guarantees ranking, citations, traffic, or sales.
- Present a business category as proof of licensure or regulatory approval.
- Present one jurisdiction's retail rules as universal.
- Claim real-time inventory accuracy unless supported by a current source.
- Publish private customer, employee, or patient information.
- Publish credentials, access tokens, or non-public operational records.
- Imply regulator certification or endorsement without evidence.

======================================================================
4. DISPENSARY BUSINESS TYPES
======================================================================

Use one or more descriptive business types where applicable:

- Cannabis dispensary
- Adult-use cannabis retailer
- Medical cannabis dispensary
- Medical and adult-use retailer
- Cannabis retail chain
- Independent cannabis retailer
- Cannabis retail cooperative, where recognized
- Cannabis retail franchise, where applicable
- Cannabis retail operator

These labels are descriptive. The publisher SHOULD separately identify
any applicable license type and issuing jurisdiction when verified.

======================================================================
5. RETAIL LOCATION MODEL
======================================================================

A retail business may operate multiple physical locations. Describe each
location separately when its address, hours, access, services, or license
differs.

Common location attributes include:

- Business name
- Public-facing store name
- Parent organization
- Brand
- Location identifier (publisher-defined, non-sensitive)
- Public street address
- Locality, region, postal code, and country
- Public telephone number
- Public contact page or email
- Website URL
- Menu URL
- Directions URL
- Geographic coordinates, when intentionally published
- Time zone (IANA identifier)
- Regular business hours
- Holiday or temporary hours
- Public accessibility information
- Parking and transit information
- Storefront status (open, temporarily closed, permanently closed,
  coming soon, or unknown)

Do not expose non-public entrances, security arrangements, staff schedules,
or sensitive location details.

======================================================================
6. RETAIL ACCESS AND CUSTOMER ELIGIBILITY
======================================================================

Describe access requirements only as applicable to the stated jurisdiction
and retail operation. Possible descriptors include:

- Adult-use access
- Medical access
- Medical patient registration required
- Government-issued identification required
- Age verification required
- Visitor check-in required
- Appointment recommended
- Appointment required
- Walk-in service available
- Online pre-order available
- In-store shopping available
- Curbside pickup available, where lawful
- Accessibility accommodations

Do not state a universal minimum age. Age, patient, identification, and
purchase requirements vary by jurisdiction and may change.

======================================================================
7. PRODUCT AND MENU VOCABULARY
======================================================================

A dispensary menu may describe products using the shared product
vocabulary in cannabis.txt and more specific retail categories, including:

- Flower
- Pre-roll
- Vaporizer product
- Concentrate
- Edible
- Beverage
- Tincture
- Capsule or tablet
- Topical
- Transdermal product
- Accessory
- Other regulated retail product

Publishers may add product subcategories. Product names and descriptions
should be factual and attributable to the source.

Where available and lawful to publish, menu entries may include:

- Product name
- Brand
- Product category
- Product format
- Package size or unit
- Publicly listed price and currency
- Applicable taxes or tax-not-included notice
- THC/CBD content as displayed by the source
- Batch or lot reference only when public and appropriate
- Laboratory or certificate-of-analysis URL, if public
- Menu item URL
- Availability status
- Last verified or last updated timestamp

Do not infer potency, effects, medical benefit, safety, or product quality.
Do not imply a menu listing means the item is currently in stock.

======================================================================
8. INVENTORY AND AVAILABILITY
======================================================================

Inventory descriptions SHOULD distinguish among:

- In stock
- Limited availability
- Out of stock
- Available for pre-order
- Coming soon
- Discontinued
- Availability unknown

Inventory changes frequently. If availability is published, identify its
source and last-updated time. Do not represent cached, estimated, or
publisher-submitted information as live point-of-sale inventory.

A menu may be a static catalog, periodically refreshed feed, or live
inventory integration. Describe the actual update method when known.

======================================================================
9. RETAIL SERVICES
======================================================================

Publicly offered services may include:

- In-store consultation or product education
- Online menu browsing
- Online ordering or pre-order
- In-store pickup
- Curbside pickup, where lawful
- Loyalty or rewards program
- Discount or promotion program
- Accessibility support
- Customer support
- Delivery (cross-reference cannabis-delivery.txt)

Describe eligibility, restrictions, and availability without implying that
any service is lawful in every jurisdiction.

======================================================================
10. LICENSE AND JURISDICTION CONTEXT
======================================================================

For each licensing claim, identify where known:

- License or authorization type
- License number, only if public
- Issuing regulator or authority
- Jurisdiction (country, state/province, locality as applicable)
- License status as reported by the cited source
- Effective date or date checked
- Public regulator lookup URL

Use status labels such as verified from regulator source, publisher-stated,
third-party-reported, expired, suspended, pending, or unknown only when the
source supports the description. A publisher's statement is not regulator
verification.

Never infer that a business is licensed from its name, address, menu, or
presence in a directory.

Publishers SHOULD express licensing claims using the standardized
Licensing record structure and the License Status and Verification
Status vocabularies defined in cannabis.txt section 8. Public
Verification URL and Date Checked are legacy names for Source URL and
Last Verified. Use Location ID to associate a license with a specific
storefront when locations are described separately.

======================================================================
11. DATA QUALITY AND FRESHNESS
======================================================================

Publishers SHOULD distinguish:

- Business- or operator-submitted information
- Information copied from an official public source
- Third-party directory information
- Estimated or inferred information
- Unknown or unverified information

Recommended timestamps use ISO 8601. Include a timezone or UTC offset
where time-of-day precision matters. Keep closed locations, changed hours,
expired promotions, and stale menus from appearing current.

======================================================================
12. PRIVACY AND SAFETY
======================================================================

Publish public business information only. Do not publish customer identities,
patient status, purchase histories, order details, personal contact data,
precise employee schedules, or other private information.

Do not include instructions that facilitate evasion of age checks, purchase
limits, licensing controls, or other legal requirements.

======================================================================
13. RELATIONSHIPS AND CROSS-REFERENCES
======================================================================

A dispensary may be related to:

- Parent company or retail operator
- One or more storefront locations
- Cannabis brands and product manufacturers
- Distributors and wholesalers
- Testing laboratories
- Menu or point-of-sale technology providers
- Delivery operators
- Events and community organizations

Use stable public URLs where available. Distinguish ownership, operation,
brand affiliation, partnership, and simple listing relationships.

If the dispensary offers or coordinates delivery, see cannabis-delivery.txt.
For brand and product information beyond retail listings, see
cannabis-flower-brand.txt, cannabis-edible-brand.txt, or
cannabis-concentrate-brand.txt as applicable. For general industry terminology,
see cannabis.txt.

======================================================================
14. RECOMMENDED PUBLISHER FIELDS
======================================================================

The following is a recommended plain-text structure, not a mandatory
machine-readable schema:

Title: [Cannabis Dispensary Knowledge File]
Version: 1.2.0
Status: [Proposed / Published]
Publisher: [Organization]
Canonical URL: [URL]
Last-Updated: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Language: en
Depends on: cannabis.txt version 1.1.0 or compatible
Contact: [Public contact URL]
Business Name: [Name]
Business Type: [Type]
Website: [URL]
Menu: [Menu URL]
Delivery Information: [URL, if applicable]
Jurisdictions: [Applicable jurisdictions]
Information Source: [Publisher / official source / third party]

A business with multiple storefronts SHOULD describe each location with a
repeating Locations block. Each location entry may carry the attributes
described in Section 5:

Locations:
- Location ID: [Publisher-defined, non-sensitive identifier]
  Name: [Public store name]
  Address: [Public street address]
  Locality: [City, region, postal code, country]
  Time Zone: [IANA identifier]
  Hours: [Public hours or hours URL]
  Menu: [Location menu URL]
  Phone: [Public telephone number]
  Store Status: [Open / temporarily closed / permanently closed /
   coming soon / unknown]
  License Reference: [Jurisdiction and public source, if applicable]

Publishers may extend this structure with clearly named fields.

======================================================================
15. VERSIONING AND GOVERNANCE
======================================================================

This extension follows the project's semantic versioning approach:

- MAJOR: incompatible changes to definitions or required fields.
- MINOR: backward-compatible additions.
- PATCH: clarifications and corrections.

The extension is community-oriented and may be implemented without
permission, payment, or use of Find420 services.
