======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

cannabis-flower-brand.txt is a proposed, human-readable extension for
describing brands whose public catalog includes cannabis flower products.

It defines shared vocabulary for brand identity, flower product formats,
publicly stated sourcing and cultivation information, product attributes,
testing references, and relationships among brands, manufacturers, and
retailers.

This extension complements cannabis.txt. It does not replace product
labels, official brand websites, menus, inventory systems, laboratory
records, APIs, or regulatory sources.

Publication does not guarantee search ranking, AI retrieval, product
availability, licensing, product quality, or regulatory compliance.

======================================================================
2. SCOPE
======================================================================

This extension covers public information about brands that market
cannabis flower products, including:

- Dried or cured cannabis flower
- Whole flower and ground flower formats
- Pre-rolls when the brand identifies them as flower products
- Cultivar, strain, or product-line names as used by the source
- Brand ownership, licensing, manufacturing, and distribution relationships
- Publicly stated product attributes and testing references

This extension describes brand and product information. It does not
provide cultivation, consumption, medical, or legal advice, and it does
not establish that a product is lawful or safe.

======================================================================
3. CONFORMANCE
======================================================================

A compatible file SHOULD:

- Be published as UTF-8 plain text at /cannabis-flower-brand.txt.
- Identify version, status, publisher, canonical URL, and last-updated date.
- Declare the compatible cannabis.txt parent version.
- Identify whether the publisher is the brand owner, an authorized
  representative, a retailer, or an independent source.
- Distinguish brand-level information from product- and batch-level facts.
- Link to public product pages and authoritative sources where available.
- Identify jurisdiction and date for legal or licensing statements.
- Mark unknown, estimated, and time-sensitive information clearly.

A compatible file MUST NOT:

- Claim that publication guarantees ranking, citations, traffic, or sales.
- Present a cultivar name as proof of genetics, lineage, or consistent
  effects without reliable supporting evidence.
- Infer potency, effects, safety, quality, or medical benefit.
- Describe a product as tested, certified, organic, or compliant without
  a source that supports the specific claim.
- Publish private customer, patient, employee, or non-public business data.

======================================================================
4. BRAND AND PRODUCT ENTITIES
======================================================================

Describe these entities separately when they are not the same:

- Brand: the public-facing name used to identify a product line.
- Brand owner: the organization that owns or controls the brand.
- Licensee: an organization authorized to use the brand under an agreement.
- Manufacturer or processor: the organization responsible for making or
  packaging a product, where known.
- Cultivator: the organization responsible for growing the source crop,
  where known.
- Distributor: the organization responsible for distribution, where known.
- Product: a named flower item, format, or package.
- Batch or lot: a production unit with its own identifiers and records.

Do not imply that a brand owns, cultivates, manufactures, or distributes
a product unless the relationship is supported by a public source.

======================================================================
5. FLOWER PRODUCT VOCABULARY
======================================================================

Descriptive product formats may include:

- Whole flower
- Ground flower
- Small buds or popcorn flower
- Pre-roll
- Multi-pack pre-roll
- Infused pre-roll (cross-reference the relevant product extension)
- Other flower format (describe)

Publishers may identify a product as indoor, greenhouse, outdoor, or
another cultivation descriptor only when the source supports that claim.
These descriptors are not quality ratings.

Cultivar names may be presented as the publisher or label states them.
Terms such as indica, sativa, hybrid, and strain are descriptive labels
whose use and meaning may vary. Do not treat them as guaranteed predictors
of effects or as verified genetic classifications.

======================================================================
6. PUBLIC BRAND INFORMATION
======================================================================

Brand-level information may include:

- Brand name and public aliases
- Brand website and public contact page
- Brand owner or responsible organization
- Public statement of the brand's role in the supply chain
- Publicly stated markets and jurisdictions
- Public brand history or positioning, attributed to the publisher
- Public product catalog or retailer menu links
- Accessibility and language options for public materials

Keep factual descriptions distinct from marketing language, opinions, and
third-party statements.

======================================================================
7. PRODUCT ATTRIBUTES
======================================================================

Where published by a reliable source, a product entry may include:

- Product name and public product URL
- Brand and product format
- Cultivar or strain name as labeled
- Net weight and package units
- Publicly stated cannabinoid values and units
- Publicly stated terpene values and units
- Cultivation or processing descriptors
- Ingredient or additive information, if applicable
- Packaging date, batch identifier, or lot identifier when public
- Manufacturer, cultivator, and distributor attribution
- Publicly listed price and currency, if relevant
- Availability status and last-verified timestamp

Report values as stated by the source, including units and whether they
refer to the product, serving, or package. Do not calculate or infer a
value from incomplete data.

======================================================================
8. TESTING AND PRODUCT CLAIMS
======================================================================

If a certificate of analysis or other test record is linked, identify the
laboratory, tested batch or lot when publicly available, report date, and
source URL. A link to one batch's report does not establish results for
other batches or future products.

Distinguish laboratory-reported results from brand statements and third-
party claims. Do not describe a test result as a guarantee of safety,
purity, efficacy, or legal compliance.

Claims about aroma, taste, appearance, effects, or quality should be
attributed to their source and should not be represented as universal
experience or medical fact.

======================================================================
9. AVAILABILITY AND RETAIL
======================================================================

Availability may be described as in stock, limited, out of stock,
discontinued, coming soon, or unknown. Identify the source and update time.
Do not imply real-time inventory unless the source supports it.

A brand may offer products in multiple markets. Describe each market
separately when availability, product catalog, distribution, or pricing
differs. For each market, identify where known:

- Jurisdiction (country, state or province, locality as applicable)
- Publicly stated availability status
- Market-specific catalog, menu, or retailer URL
- Responsible distributor or licensee for that market, if public
- Source and last-verified date

A brand may link to retailers or menus that carry its products. Distinguish
retailer listings from brand-operated sales channels. Retail, delivery,
promotion, and purchase rules vary by jurisdiction. See cannabis-dispensary.txt
and cannabis-delivery.txt where applicable.

======================================================================
10. LICENSE AND JURISDICTION CONTEXT
======================================================================

For a public licensing or authorization statement, provide where known:

- The organization and activity the authorization applies to
- License or authorization type and number, only if public
- Issuing authority and jurisdiction
- Status as reported by the cited source
- Effective date or date checked
- Official public lookup URL

A brand's presence in a catalog, retailer menu, or directory does not
prove that the brand or any supply-chain participant is authorized.

Publishers SHOULD express licensing claims using the standardized
Licensing record structure and the License Status and Verification
Status vocabularies defined in cannabis.txt section 8. Identify the
correct License Holder: a brand, licensee, cultivator, processor,
manufacturer, or distributor may each hold separate authorizations, and
a brand relying on a contract manufacturer's license should name the
manufacturer as the holder. Public Verification URL and Date Checked
are legacy names for Source URL and Last Verified.

======================================================================
11. DATA QUALITY AND FRESHNESS
======================================================================

Identify whether information is brand-submitted, copied from an official
source, reported by a retailer, or provided by another third party.
Use ISO 8601 dates where practical. Product formulations, labels, batch
records, prices, and availability may change; date each time-sensitive
claim and correct stale information promptly.

======================================================================
12. PRIVACY AND SAFETY
======================================================================

Publish public business and product information only. Do not publish
customer identities, purchase histories, patient status, private employee
details, or non-public production and security records.

Do not include instructions that facilitate evasion of age checks,
purchase limits, product safeguards, or applicable law.

======================================================================
13. RELATIONSHIPS AND CROSS-REFERENCES
======================================================================

Describe ownership, licensing, manufacturing, cultivation, distribution,
retail, and event relationships explicitly and link to public sources.
Do not imply endorsement from a simple listing or event appearance.

For general industry terminology, see cannabis.txt. For retail context,
see cannabis-dispensary.txt. For delivery context, see
cannabis-delivery.txt.

Publishers may extend this structure with clearly named fields.

======================================================================
15. VERSIONING AND GOVERNANCE
======================================================================

This extension follows semantic versioning:

- MAJOR: incompatible changes to definitions or required fields.
- MINOR: backward-compatible additions.
- PATCH: clarifications and corrections.

The extension is community-oriented and may be implemented without
permission, payment, or use of Find420 services.
