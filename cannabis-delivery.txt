======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

cannabis-delivery.txt is a proposed, human-readable extension for
 describing cannabis delivery businesses, delivery services, and the
 public-facing processes used to receive and fulfill delivery orders.

It defines shared terminology for delivery operators, service areas,
ordering channels, fulfillment models, dispatch, delivery windows,
customer handoff, delivery status, and jurisdictional restrictions.

This extension complements cannabis.txt. It does not replace official
business websites, ordering systems, point-of-sale platforms, dispatch
software, APIs, structured data, or regulatory records.

Publication does not guarantee search ranking, AI retrieval, business
verification, licensing, or inclusion in any directory or recommendation.

======================================================================
2. SCOPE
======================================================================

This extension covers public information about cannabis delivery activity,
including:

- Licensed cannabis delivery operators, where authorized
- Dispensaries that provide their own delivery service
- Third-party delivery arrangements, where lawful
- Online ordering and order submission channels
- Delivery service areas and geographic restrictions
- Order acceptance and fulfillment workflows
- Dispatch and delivery windows
- Pickup or handoff options associated with delivery operations
- Public delivery fees, minimums, and service policies
- Delivery status descriptions and customer support

A business may operate both a retail storefront and a delivery service.
Publish cannabis-dispensary.txt as well when describing its storefront
retail operations.

This file describes public business information; it does not establish
that a business, driver, vehicle, or delivery transaction is authorized.

======================================================================
3. CONFORMANCE
======================================================================

A compatible file SHOULD:

- Be published at /cannabis-delivery.txt on the publisher's domain.
- Be served as UTF-8 plain text (text/plain; charset=utf-8).
- Identify version, status, publisher, canonical URL, and last-updated date.
- Declare the compatible cannabis.txt parent version.
- Identify whether the publisher is the operator, retailer, platform,
  technology provider, or third-party directory.
- Use shared vocabulary from cannabis.txt where practical.
- Link to public ordering, service-area, policy, and licensing pages.
- Identify the source and freshness of service-area and availability data.
- Qualify legal and licensing statements by jurisdiction and effective date.

A compatible file MUST NOT:

- Claim that publication guarantees ranking, citations, traffic, or sales.
- Present a business category as proof of licensure or authorization.
- Present one jurisdiction's delivery rules as universal.
- Claim real-time order or driver status without a live authoritative source.
- Publish customer identities, order histories, delivery addresses, or
  other private transaction information.
- Publish driver personal details, schedules, routes, or credentials.
- Include access tokens, credentials, or non-public operational records.
- Imply regulator certification or endorsement without evidence.

======================================================================
4. DELIVERY BUSINESS AND SERVICE TYPES
======================================================================

Use one or more descriptive types where applicable:

- Cannabis delivery operator
- Dispensary-operated delivery service
- Retailer-managed delivery
- Third-party delivery service
- Delivery technology platform
- Order marketplace or ordering platform
- Contracted logistics provider, where lawful
- Medical cannabis delivery service, where authorized
- Adult-use cannabis delivery service, where authorized

These categories describe business functions, not legal authorization.
A technology platform or marketplace should not be described as the
licensed seller or delivery operator unless that is factually accurate.

======================================================================
5. DELIVERY SERVICE MODEL
======================================================================

Describe how the service is operated, where publicly known:

- Retailer-owned and operated
- Operator-owned and operated
- Third-party contracted fulfillment
- Marketplace connecting customers and retailers
- Platform providing ordering technology only
- Hybrid or multi-operator model

Identify the responsible seller and delivery operator separately when
they are different entities. Do not imply that an ordering platform
possesses inventory, sells products, or performs delivery if it does not.

======================================================================
6. ORDERING CHANNELS
======================================================================

Public ordering channels may include:

- Business website
- Online menu and checkout
- Mobile application
- Telephone ordering
- In-store order with delivery fulfillment
- Authorized third-party ordering platform

Where available, publish:

- Public ordering URL
- Menu URL
- Supported ordering channel
- Order acceptance hours
- Whether pre-orders are supported
- Whether account creation is required
- Publicly stated payment methods
- Order minimum, if applicable
- Publicly stated delivery fee
- Cancellation and refund policy URL

Do not publish private account, payment, or customer information.

======================================================================
7. SERVICE AREA AND COVERAGE
======================================================================

Describe service coverage at a level appropriate for public discovery:

- Country
- State or province
- County or regional area
- City or municipality
- Publicly named neighborhoods
- Postal codes or postal-code ranges, where appropriate
- Publicly named service city or market
- Publicly named hub or operating base, at city or regional level only
- Published coverage map URL

An operator may serve multiple cities or dispatch orders from multiple
hubs. Describe each service city or hub separately when coverage, ordering
channels, hours, fees, or eligibility differ. A hub SHOULD be identified
at city or regional level only; do not publish addresses or locations of
facilities that are not open to the public.

State whether service areas are confirmed by the operator, supplied by a
third party, estimated, or unknown. Identify last-updated time where known.

Do not expose individual delivery addresses, customer locations, driver
GPS traces, private route plans, or sensitive operational boundaries.

Service availability may depend on the customer's address, operating
hours, inventory, order eligibility, and local rules. A published service
area is not a guarantee that a particular order can be fulfilled.

======================================================================
8. DELIVERY ELIGIBILITY AND JURISDICTION
======================================================================

Eligibility requirements vary by jurisdiction and may include:

- Age verification
- Medical patient or caregiver eligibility
- Valid identification
- Address within an authorized service area
- Recipient identity verification at handoff
- Purchase or possession limits
- Order-time restrictions
- Delivery-time restrictions
- Other regulator-defined requirements

Describe only requirements supported by the applicable authority or
operator policy. Identify the jurisdiction and source. Do not provide a
universal age, purchase limit, or delivery rule.

======================================================================
9. ORDER FULFILLMENT AND DISPATCH
======================================================================

A delivery workflow may include these descriptive stages:

- Cart or order submitted
- Order received
- Order under review
- Eligibility check pending
- Order accepted
- Order rejected or cancelled
- Preparing order
- Awaiting dispatch
- Assigned for delivery
- Out for delivery
- Delivery attempted
- Awaiting customer handoff
- Delivered
- Returned or undeliverable
- Refunded or otherwise resolved

These are vocabulary terms, not mandatory system states. Publishers may
use different internal workflows and map them to the closest public term.
Do not expose internal dispatch data or private transaction records.

Where relevant, describe:

- Typical preparation window
- Published delivery window
- Whether scheduled delivery is offered
- Whether same-day delivery is offered
- Whether delivery tracking is available
- Whether customer notifications are provided
- Public procedure for failed or undeliverable orders

Avoid presenting estimates as guarantees.

======================================================================
10. DELIVERY HANDOFF
======================================================================

Publicly describe applicable handoff methods and requirements, such as:

- Recipient identity verification
- Age or patient eligibility verification
- Recipient must be present
- Signature or acknowledgement required
- Delivery to an authorized recipient only
- No unattended delivery, where applicable
- Failed delivery and return procedure

Actual requirements must be tied to the applicable jurisdiction and
operator policy. Do not describe methods that would bypass required
identity, age, or eligibility checks.

======================================================================
11. FEES, TIMING, AND SERVICE POLICIES
======================================================================

Where publicly available, a publisher may identify:

- Delivery fee and currency
- Minimum order amount
- Fee waiver threshold
- Published delivery hours
- Estimated delivery window
- Scheduled delivery availability
- Service interruptions
- Cancellation policy
- Refund and return policy
- Customer support channel

Clearly distinguish fixed fees from estimates, promotions, and conditional
charges. Include a source URL and last-updated date where practical.

======================================================================
12. LICENSE AND JURISDICTION CONTEXT
======================================================================

For each licensing or authorization claim, identify where known:

- License or authorization type
- License number, only if public
- Licensed legal entity
- Issuing regulator or authority
- Jurisdiction (country, state/province, locality as applicable)
- Authorized activity (retail, delivery, transport, or other)
- License status as reported by the cited source
- Effective date or date checked
- Public regulator lookup URL

Distinguish the retailer, seller, delivery operator, platform, and any
contracted logistics provider. One entity's authorization must not be
attributed to another entity without evidence.

Use labels such as verified from regulator source, publisher-stated,
third-party-reported, expired, suspended, pending, or unknown only when
the cited source supports the description. Never infer authorization from
an app listing, website, menu, vehicle, or directory entry.

======================================================================
13. DATA QUALITY, FRESHNESS, AND STATUS
======================================================================

Publishers SHOULD distinguish:

- Operator-submitted service information
- Information from an official public source
- Third-party directory information
- Estimated coverage or timing
- Unknown or unverified information

Use ISO 8601 timestamps for update times. Identify time zones using IANA
time-zone identifiers when publishing local operating hours or windows.
Keep service areas, fees, operating hours, and policies current.

A service may be described as active, temporarily unavailable, paused,
coming soon, discontinued, or unknown when supported by the source.

======================================================================
14. PRIVACY AND SECURITY
======================================================================

This extension is for public business information only. Never publish:

- Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, or delivery addresses
- Order contents linked to an identifiable customer
- Patient status or medical records
- Payment details or account credentials
- Driver identities where not already intentionally public
- Driver schedules, live locations, or route histories
- Security procedures or sensitive operational details

Use aggregated or generalized information where appropriate.

======================================================================
15. RELATIONSHIPS AND CROSS-REFERENCES
======================================================================

A delivery service may be related to:

- A cannabis dispensary or retailer
- A licensed delivery operator
- A parent company or retail chain
- A cannabis brand or product supplier
- An ordering or menu platform
- A payment provider
- A logistics or technology provider
- A service-area or local business directory

Distinguish ownership, operation, fulfillment, technology provision,
contractual relationships, and simple directory listings.

If the operator also runs storefront retail locations, see
cannabis-dispensary.txt. For general industry terminology, see cannabis.txt.

======================================================================
16. RECOMMENDED PUBLISHER FIELDS
======================================================================

The following is a recommended plain-text structure, not a mandatory
machine-readable schema:

Title: [Cannabis Delivery Knowledge File]
Version: 1.1.0
Status: [Proposed / Published]
Publisher: [Organization]
Canonical URL: [URL]
Last-Updated: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Language: en
Depends on: cannabis.txt version 1.0.0 or compatible
Contact: [Public contact URL]
Business Name: [Name]
Business Type: [Type]
Seller: [Legal or public business name]
Delivery Operator: [Name]
Ordering URL: [URL]
Menu URL: [URL]
Delivery Hours: [Public hours URL or description]
Delivery Policy: [URL]
Jurisdictions: [Applicable jurisdictions]
Information Source: [Operator / official source / third party]

An operator serving multiple cities or operating multiple hubs SHOULD
describe each area with a repeating Service Areas block:

Service Areas:
- Service City or Region: [Publicly named area]
  Hub City: [Publicly named operating base, city level, if applicable]
  Ordering URL: [URL for this area, if it differs]
  Delivery Hours: [Hours or URL for this area, if it differs]
  Coverage Source: [Operator-confirmed / third-party / estimated]
  Last Verified: [YYYY-MM-DD]

Publishers may extend this structure with clearly named fields.

17. VERSIONING AND GOVERNANCE
======================================================================

This extension follows the project's semantic versioning approach:

- MAJOR: incompatible changes to definitions or required fields.
- MINOR: backward-compatible additions.
- PATCH: clarifications and corrections.

The extension is community-oriented and may be implemented without
permission, payment, or use of Find420 services.
