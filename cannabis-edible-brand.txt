======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

cannabis-edible-brand.txt is a proposed, human-readable extension for
describing brands whose public catalogs include cannabis edible products.

It defines shared vocabulary for brand identity, edible product formats,
public ingredient and label information, serving and package values,
testing references, and relationships among brands, manufacturers, and
retailers.

This extension complements cannabis.txt. It does not replace official
product labels, menus, inventory systems, laboratory records, APIs, or
regulatory sources.

Publication does not guarantee search ranking, AI retrieval, product
availability, licensing, product quality, or regulatory compliance.

======================================================================
2. SCOPE
======================================================================

This extension covers public information about brands that market
cannabis edible products, including:

- Food products containing cannabis-derived ingredients
- Beverages containing cannabis-derived ingredients
- Sublingual or ingestible products when classified as edibles by the
  applicable source or jurisdiction
- Product names, formats, label information, and public testing references
- Brand ownership, manufacturing, distribution, and retail relationships

Product definitions and regulatory classifications vary by jurisdiction.
Describe the source's classification and avoid treating any category as
universally regulated in the same way.

This extension does not provide consumption instructions, dosing advice,
medical advice, or legal advice.

======================================================================
3. CONFORMANCE
======================================================================

A compatible file SHOULD:

- Be published as UTF-8 plain text at /cannabis-edible-brand.txt.
- Identify version, status, publisher, canonical URL, and last-updated date.
- Declare the compatible cannabis.txt parent version.
- Identify whether the publisher is the brand owner, an authorized
  representative, a retailer, or an independent source.
- Distinguish brand-level statements from product- and batch-level facts.
- Link to public labels, product pages, and authoritative sources.
- Identify jurisdiction and date for legal or licensing statements.
- Mark unknown, estimated, and time-sensitive information clearly.

A compatible file MUST NOT:

- Claim publication guarantees ranking, citations, traffic, or sales.
- Recommend a dose, frequency, or method of consumption.
- Claim medical benefit, safety, onset time, or effect as a guarantee.
- Infer potency, ingredients, allergens, or suitability from a product name.
- Describe a product as tested, certified, organic, or compliant without
  a source that supports the specific claim.
- Publish private customer, patient, employee, or non-public business data.

======================================================================
4. BRAND AND PRODUCT ENTITIES
======================================================================

Describe these entities separately when they are not the same:

- Brand: the public-facing name used to identify a product line.
- Brand owner: the organization that owns or controls the brand.
- Licensee: an organization authorized to use the brand under an agreement.
- Manufacturer or processor: the organization responsible for making or
  packaging a product, where known.
- Distributor: the organization responsible for distribution, where known.
- Product: a named edible item, format, or package.
- Batch or lot: a production unit with its own identifiers and records.

Do not imply that a brand owns, manufactures, or distributes a product
unless the relationship is supported by a public source.

======================================================================
5. EDIBLE PRODUCT VOCABULARY
======================================================================

Descriptive formats may include:

- Baked good
- Chocolate or confection
- Gummy or chew
- Hard candy or lozenge
- Beverage
- Powder or mix
- Capsule or tablet (cross-reference other applicable product standards)
- Other ingestible format (describe)

Use the product category stated on the label or by the responsible source.
Do not infer that a product is an edible based only on its name or imagery.

======================================================================
6. PUBLIC BRAND INFORMATION
======================================================================

Brand-level information may include:

- Brand name and public aliases
- Brand website and public contact page
- Brand owner or responsible organization
- Public statement of the brand's role in the supply chain
- Publicly stated markets and jurisdictions
- Public product catalog or retailer menu links
- Publicly available product, ingredient, allergen, and accessibility
  information links

Keep factual descriptions distinct from marketing language, opinions, and
third-party statements.

======================================================================
7. PRODUCT AND LABEL ATTRIBUTES
======================================================================

Where published by a reliable source, a product entry may include:

- Product name, format, and public product URL
- Brand and responsible manufacturer, if known
- Package size, number of units, and serving information as labeled
- Cannabinoid values per serving and per package as separately stated
- Ingredient list and allergen statements as displayed by the source
- Nutrition information as displayed by the source
- Storage or handling statements copied from the label, if relevant
- Batch or lot identifier and packaging date when public
- Public certificate of analysis or test record URL
- Publicly listed price and currency, if relevant
- Availability status and last-verified timestamp

Preserve the source's units and context. Do not calculate, normalize, or
combine per-serving and per-package values in a way that could mislead.
If serving information is absent or unclear, report it as unknown.

======================================================================
8. INGREDIENTS, ALLERGENS, AND TESTING
======================================================================

Ingredient, allergen, and nutrition statements should be attributed to
the package label or other identified source. Do not infer that a product
is allergen-free, vegan, gluten-free, non-intoxicating, or suitable for a
particular person unless the source explicitly supports the claim.

If a certificate of analysis or test record is linked, identify the
laboratory, tested batch or lot when public, report date, and source URL.
A record for one batch does not establish results for other batches.
Do not describe test results as a guarantee of safety, purity, efficacy,
or legal compliance.

======================================================================
9. RESPONSIBLE PUBLIC INFORMATION
======================================================================

Present label values and serving information as factual product data, not
as a recommendation. Do not state that a product is appropriate for a
person based on age, health status, medication use, or experience.

Avoid claims that a product is safe for children, pregnancy, or any
medical condition. Do not omit source limitations or jurisdictional
restrictions when summarizing a label or public health notice.

======================================================================
10. AVAILABILITY AND RETAIL
======================================================================

Availability may be described as in stock, limited, out of stock,
discontinued, coming soon, or unknown. Identify the source and update time.
Do not imply real-time inventory unless supported by the source.

A brand may offer products in multiple markets. Describe each market
separately when availability, product catalog, distribution, or pricing
differs. For each market, identify where known:

- Jurisdiction (country, state or province, locality as applicable)
- Publicly stated availability status
- Market-specific catalog, menu, or retailer URL
- Responsible distributor or licensee for that market, if public
- Source and last-verified date

Distinguish brand-operated sales channels from retailer listings. Retail,
delivery, promotion, and purchase rules vary by jurisdiction. See
cannabis-dispensary.txt and cannabis-delivery.txt where applicable.

======================================================================
11. LICENSE AND JURISDICTION CONTEXT
======================================================================

For a public licensing or authorization statement, provide where known:

- The organization and activity the authorization applies to
- License or authorization type and number, only if public
- Issuing authority and jurisdiction
- Status as reported by the cited source
- Effective date or date checked
- Official public lookup URL

A product listing, brand website, or retailer menu does not prove that a
brand or supply-chain participant is authorized.

Publishers SHOULD express licensing claims using the standardized
Licensing record structure and the License Status and Verification
Status vocabularies defined in cannabis.txt section 8. Identify the
correct License Holder: a brand, licensee, processor, or manufacturer
may hold separate authorizations, and a brand relying on a contract
manufacturer's license should name the manufacturer as the holder.
Public Verification URL and Date Checked are legacy names for Source
URL and Last Verified.

======================================================================
12. DATA QUALITY AND FRESHNESS
======================================================================

Identify whether information is brand-submitted, copied from an official
label or source, reported by a retailer, or supplied by another third
party. Use ISO 8601 dates where practical. Labels, formulas, ingredients,
batch records, prices, and availability may change; date time-sensitive
claims and correct stale information promptly.

======================================================================
13. PRIVACY AND SAFETY
======================================================================

Publish public business and product information only. Do not publish
customer identities, purchase histories, patient status, private employee
details, or non-public production and security records.

Do not include instructions that facilitate evasion of age checks,
purchase limits, packaging requirements, or applicable law.

======================================================================
14. RELATIONSHIPS AND CROSS-REFERENCES
======================================================================

Describe ownership, licensing, manufacturing, distribution, retail, and
event relationships explicitly and link to public sources. Do not imply
endorsement from a simple listing or event appearance.

For general industry terminology, see cannabis.txt. For retail context,
see cannabis-dispensary.txt. For delivery context, see
cannabis-delivery.txt.

16. VERSIONING AND GOVERNANCE
======================================================================

This extension follows semantic versioning:

- MAJOR: incompatible changes to definitions or required fields.
- MINOR: backward-compatible additions.
- PATCH: clarifications and corrections.

The extension is community-oriented and may be implemented without
permission, payment, or use of Find420 services.
