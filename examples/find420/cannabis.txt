======================================================================
1. ABOUT FIND420.EVENTS
======================================================================

Find420.Events is a public event discovery platform for cannabis-related
events, 420 events, pop-ups, festivals, and community experiences.
The platform also publishes public information about cannabis brands,
locations, education, and related tools.

This file describes Find420.Events as a publisher and platform. It is
not the cannabis.txt specification. The open specification is published
at https://find420.events/specs/cannabis.txt.

======================================================================
2. PLATFORM ROLE
======================================================================

Find420.Events helps people discover public events and related
organizations. Event detail pages may contain information submitted by
organizers or other publishers. A listing on Find420.Events does not by
itself establish that an event, organizer, venue, claim, or admission
offer has been independently verified.

Find420.Events is not necessarily the organizer, venue operator, ticket
seller, or source of every event shown on the platform. Refer to the
event organizer's official information for current details.
