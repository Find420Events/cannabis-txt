======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

cannabis-concentrate-brand.txt is a proposed, human-readable extension for
describing brands whose public catalogs include cannabis concentrates.

It defines shared vocabulary for brand identity, concentrate product
formats, publicly stated composition and sourcing, testing references,
and relationships among brands, processors, manufacturers, and retailers.

This extension complements cannabis.txt. It does not replace official
product labels, menus, inventory systems, laboratory records, APIs, or
regulatory sources.

Publication does not guarantee search ranking, AI retrieval, product
availability, licensing, product quality, or regulatory compliance.

======================================================================
2. SCOPE
======================================================================

This extension covers public information about brands that market
cannabis concentrate products, including:

- Extracts and other concentrated cannabis products
- Solvent-based and solventless product categories, as named by the source
- Cartridge, vaporizer, dab, or other concentrate formats when applicable
- Product names, label information, and public testing references
- Brand ownership, processing, manufacturing, distribution, and retail
  relationships

Product definitions, permitted formats, and regulated activities vary
by jurisdiction. Describe the source's terminology without treating a
category as universally regulated in the same way.

This extension is descriptive. It does not provide extraction, processing,
consumption, or medical instructions.

======================================================================
3. CONFORMANCE
======================================================================

A compatible file SHOULD:

- Be published as UTF-8 plain text at /cannabis-concentrate-brand.txt.
- Identify version, status, publisher, canonical URL, and last-updated date.
- Declare the compatible cannabis.txt parent version.
- Identify whether the publisher is the brand owner, an authorized
  representative, a retailer, or an independent source.
- Distinguish brand-level statements from product- and batch-level facts.
- Link to public product pages, labels, and authoritative sources.
- Identify jurisdiction and date for legal or licensing statements.
- Mark unknown, estimated, and time-sensitive information clearly.

A compatible file MUST NOT:

- Claim publication guarantees ranking, citations, traffic, or sales.
- Provide operational instructions for extracting, processing, or using
  concentrates.
- Infer potency, effects, purity, safety, quality, or medical benefit.
- Describe a product as tested, certified, solvent-free, or compliant
  without a source that supports the specific claim.
- Publish private customer, patient, employee, or non-public business data.

======================================================================
4. BRAND AND PRODUCT ENTITIES
======================================================================

Describe these entities separately when they are not the same:

- Brand: the public-facing name used to identify a product line.
- Brand owner: the organization that owns or controls the brand.
- Licensee: an organization authorized to use the brand under an agreement.
- Processor or manufacturer: the organization responsible for processing,
  making, or packaging a product, where known.
- Cultivator or source supplier: an organization responsible for source
  material, where known.
- Distributor: the organization responsible for distribution, where known.
- Product: a named concentrate item, format, or package.
- Batch or lot: a production unit with its own identifiers and records.

Do not infer or imply a supply-chain relationship without a public source.

======================================================================
5. CONCENTRATE PRODUCT VOCABULARY
======================================================================

Descriptive product categories may include:

- Rosin
- Resin
- Wax
- Shatter
- Badder or batter
- Crumble
- Oil
- Distillate
- Isolate
- Hash or hash-derived concentrate
- Cartridge or vaporizer product
- Infused product (cross-reference its relevant product extension)
- Other concentrate format (describe)

Use terms as stated by the product label or responsible source. These
names may not have consistent definitions across producers or regions.
Do not infer process, ingredients, quality, or composition from a category
name alone.

======================================================================
6. PUBLIC BRAND INFORMATION
======================================================================

Brand-level information may include:

- Brand name and public aliases
- Brand website and public contact page
- Brand owner or responsible organization
- Public statement of the brand's role in the supply chain
- Publicly stated markets and jurisdictions
- Public product catalog or retailer menu links
- Public sourcing, processing, and quality-control descriptions, attributed
  to the source

Keep factual descriptions distinct from marketing language, opinions, and
third-party statements.

======================================================================
7. PRODUCT ATTRIBUTES
======================================================================

Where published by a reliable source, a product entry may include:

- Product name, category, and public product URL
- Brand and responsible processor or manufacturer, if known
- Format and package size
- Cannabinoid values, units, and package or serving context
- Terpene values when published, including units and source
- Ingredients, additives, or diluents as stated by the label
- Source material or cultivar attribution, if publicly documented
- Batch or lot identifier and packaging date when public
- Public certificate of analysis or test record URL
- Publicly listed price and currency, if relevant
- Availability status and last-verified timestamp

Report values as stated by the source. Do not infer a product's contents,
potency, or intended use from marketing terms, category, or appearance.

======================================================================
8. TESTING AND COMPOSITION CLAIMS
======================================================================

If a certificate of analysis or other test record is linked, identify the
laboratory, tested batch or lot when public, report date, and source URL.
A record for one batch does not establish results for other batches.

Attribute claims such as solvent-free, pesticide-free, contaminant-free,
or full-spectrum to a specific public source and batch where applicable.
Do not treat a test record or marketing claim as a guarantee of safety,
purity, efficacy, or legal compliance.

Distinguish laboratory-reported results from brand statements and
third-party claims. Preserve units and explain whether values apply to a
sample, package, or another stated basis.

======================================================================
9. AVAILABILITY AND RETAIL
======================================================================

Availability may be described as in stock, limited, out of stock,
discontinued, coming soon, or unknown. Identify the source and update time.
Do not imply real-time inventory unless supported by the source.

Distinguish brand-operated sales channels from retailer listings. Retail,
delivery, promotion, and purchase rules vary by jurisdiction. See
cannabis-dispensary.txt and cannabis-delivery.txt where applicable.

======================================================================
10. LICENSE AND JURISDICTION CONTEXT
======================================================================

For a public licensing or authorization statement, provide where known:

- The organization and activity the authorization applies to
- License or authorization type and number, only if public
- Issuing authority and jurisdiction
- Status as reported by the cited source
- Effective date or date checked
- Official public lookup URL

A brand listing or product menu does not prove that a brand or any
supply-chain participant is authorized.

======================================================================
11. DATA QUALITY AND FRESHNESS
======================================================================

Identify whether information is brand-submitted, copied from an official
label or source, reported by a retailer, or supplied by another third
party. Use ISO 8601 dates where practical. Product formulations, labels,
batch records, prices, and availability may change; date time-sensitive
claims and correct stale information promptly.

======================================================================
12. PRIVACY AND SAFETY
======================================================================

Publish public business and product information only. Do not publish
customer identities, purchase histories, patient status, private employee
details, or non-public production and security records.

Do not include instructions that facilitate evasion of age checks,
purchase limits, product safeguards, or applicable law.

======================================================================
13. RELATIONSHIPS AND CROSS-REFERENCES
======================================================================

Describe ownership, licensing, processing, manufacturing, distribution,
retail, and event relationships explicitly and link to public sources.
Do not imply endorsement from a simple listing or event appearance.

For general industry terminology, see cannabis.txt. For retail context,
see cannabis-dispensary.txt. For delivery context, see
cannabis-delivery.txt.
