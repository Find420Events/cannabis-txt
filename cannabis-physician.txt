======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

cannabis-physician.txt is a proposed, human-readable extension for
describing physicians and physician practices that publicly provide
cannabis-related clinical, educational, or consultation services.

It defines shared vocabulary for public professional profiles, credentials,
practice locations, service descriptions, jurisdiction-specific
authorizations, and source attribution.

This extension complements cannabis.txt. It does not replace official
medical board records, clinic websites, appointment systems, medical
records, or regulatory sources.

Publication does not guarantee search ranking, AI retrieval, professional
qualification, licensure, patient eligibility, treatment access, or
clinical outcomes.

======================================================================
2. SCOPE
======================================================================

This extension covers public professional information about:

- Physicians who discuss cannabis in a clinical or educational context
- Practices that publicly offer cannabis-related consultations
- Publicly stated professional credentials and practice locations
- Public patient information links and appointment contact channels
- Jurisdiction-specific authorization statements, where applicable

The term "physician" should be used only when supported by a verifiable
professional credential. Other clinicians and healthcare professionals
may be described using their accurate professional titles instead.

This extension is not a medical directory verification service and does
not provide clinical guidance, diagnosis, prescribing, or legal advice.

======================================================================
3. CONFORMANCE
======================================================================

A compatible file SHOULD:

- Be published as UTF-8 plain text at /cannabis-physician.txt.
- Identify version, status, publisher, canonical URL, and last-updated date.
- Declare the compatible cannabis.txt parent version.
- Identify the physician or practice's relationship to the information.
- Use the professional title and credentials exactly as publicly verified.
- Link to official licensing or board sources when available.
- Identify jurisdictions and dates for licensing or authorization claims.
- Distinguish general education from patient-specific clinical services.
- Mark unknown, estimated, and time-sensitive information clearly.

A compatible file MUST NOT:

- Imply that publication verifies a credential, license, or medical claim.
- Guarantee eligibility, authorization, diagnosis, treatment, or outcome.
- Present cannabis as appropriate or safe for every person or condition.
- Make unsupported efficacy, safety, or cure claims.
- Publish patient identities, medical records, or private health information.
- Collect or expose sensitive personal, credential, or account data.

======================================================================
4. PROFESSIONAL AND PRACTICE ENTITIES
======================================================================

Describe these entities separately when they are not the same:

- Physician: an individual using a verified professional title.
- Practice: the organization or clinic through which services are offered.
- License: an authorization issued by a named professional authority.
- Service: a publicly described consultation, educational, or clinical
  offering.
- Location: a public practice address or service area.
- Referral source: an organization or professional listed as a referral
  contact, without implying endorsement.

A practice profile must not imply that every listed clinician provides
every service, or that the practice's authorization applies to all
locations or jurisdictions.

A physician or practice may operate at multiple public practice locations
and may offer telehealth across multiple jurisdictions. Describe each
location separately when its services, hours, appointment channels,
jurisdiction, or authorization differs. Publish only locations that are
open to patients or already intentionally public.

======================================================================
5. PROFESSIONAL PROFILE
======================================================================

Public profile information may include:

- Public professional name and title
- Public professional biography and practice website
- Degree or credential as represented by an official source
- Specialty or board certification with source and date checked
- Public professional license jurisdiction and status
- Practice name and public contact page
- Public clinic locations or telehealth availability
- Languages and accessibility information, if publicly confirmed
- Educational publications or public presentations

Do not include private home addresses, personal phone numbers, private
email addresses, birth dates, personal identifiers, or unpublished
employment details.

======================================================================
6. SERVICE VOCABULARY
======================================================================

Publicly stated service categories may include:

- General cannabis-related health education
- Clinical consultation
- Discussion of cannabis and health considerations
- Patient eligibility assessment, where permitted
- Follow-up care
- Referral to another qualified professional
- Telehealth consultation, where permitted

Describe services using the practice's own public wording. A listed
service does not establish that an individual is eligible, that a specific
product will be recommended, or that a particular outcome will occur.
Avoid implying that a physician sells or endorses a product unless this
relationship is explicitly and accurately disclosed.

======================================================================
7. CREDENTIALS AND LICENSE VERIFICATION
======================================================================

For each public credential or license claim, provide where available:

- Exact credential or professional title
- Issuing authority or educational institution
- Jurisdiction, if relevant
- Public verification source URL
- Status as displayed by the source
- Date checked

Distinguish a physician's professional license from any separate
authorization required to provide a cannabis-related service. Do not infer
one authorization from another. A directory listing, self-description, or
practice website is not independent verification.

======================================================================
8. MEDICAL CLAIMS AND EDUCATIONAL CONTENT
======================================================================

Attribute medical or scientific statements to a dated, reliable source.
Distinguish clinical evidence, professional opinion, general education,
and promotional material. Do not present preliminary findings as settled
consensus or make unsupported claims about efficacy, safety, interactions,
or suitability.

This file must not include individualized treatment recommendations,
diagnosis, dosing guidance, or instructions to start, stop, or change a
medication. Readers should seek advice from a qualified professional who
can assess their individual circumstances.

======================================================================
9. PATIENT ACCESS AND APPOINTMENTS
======================================================================

Public appointment information may include:

- Official scheduling URL
- Public phone number or contact form
- Public clinic address and operating hours
- Telehealth availability and applicable jurisdictions
- Publicly stated eligibility or documentation requirements
- Accessibility and language support

Do not guarantee that a person qualifies for a service. Requirements
depend on jurisdiction and individual circumstances. Direct readers to
the practice and relevant official authorities for current requirements.

======================================================================
10. JURISDICTION AND AUTHORIZATION CONTEXT
======================================================================

For each legal or authorization statement, identify where known:

- The physician, practice, and activity the statement applies to
- Relevant jurisdiction and issuing authority
- License or authorization status as reported by the cited source
- Effective date or date checked
- Official public source URL

Do not characterize a physician as authorized to recommend, certify,
prescribe, or provide a service unless the applicable jurisdiction's
official source supports that specific claim. Do not present one
jurisdiction's rules as universal.

======================================================================
11. DATA QUALITY AND FRESHNESS
======================================================================

Identify whether information is physician-submitted, practice-submitted,
copied from an official source, or supplied by a third party. Use ISO 8601
dates where practical. Credentials, license status, practice locations,
service availability, and jurisdictional rules may change; date these
claims and correct stale information promptly.

======================================================================
12. PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
======================================================================

Publish public professional information only. Never include patient
identities, appointment details, medical records, health conditions,
contact information, or other information that could identify a patient.

Do not collect patient information through this text file. Route any
appointment or clinical contact to the practice's own secure channels.

======================================================================
13. RELATIONSHIPS AND CROSS-REFERENCES
======================================================================

Describe practice affiliations, referrals, employment, sponsorships, and
commercial relationships accurately. Disclose material relationships
where relevant. Do not imply endorsement, partnership, or referral from
a simple listing.

For general industry terminology, see cannabis.txt. For events and public
education activity, see cannabis-events.txt where applicable.

15. VERSIONING AND GOVERNANCE
======================================================================

This extension follows semantic versioning:

- MAJOR: incompatible changes to definitions or required fields.
- MINOR: backward-compatible additions.
- PATCH: clarifications and corrections.

The extension is community-oriented and may be implemented without
permission, payment, or use of Find420 services.
